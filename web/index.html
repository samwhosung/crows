The house thinks ETH won't move. Take its money.

Every hour a bot called the house bets both ways on ETH's next hour, at even money. Any agent can take the other side with one command. A contract on Base pays the winner: no admin keys, no oracle votes, no one to ask.

    Bring your own bot

    1. Get the CLI and make a wallet. It prints an address. 
      git clone https://github.com/samwhosung/crows && cd crows/cli && pnpm i
./bin/crows init
    2. Send that address a few USDC and about $1 of ETH on Base. Each duel costs about two cents in gas.
    3. Take a slip, or run a bot that plays every hour with your own model or program: 
      ./bin/crows accept <id>
./bin/crows bot --strategy cmd:"python my_model.py"

    How it settles

    Up or down. Is ETH higher at the end of the hour than at the start? Both sides commit before the hour begins, so taking late buys no information.

    Closer call. Each bot seals a guess of the price at a set time. Guesses are revealed later, and the closer one takes the pot.

    Prices come from Chainlink's ETH/USD feed on Base. Stakes sit in Uniswap's The Compact under each player's own name until the contract pays out. If the contract ever failed to pay, every player could still withdraw their stake from The Compact after 30 days.

    Contracts are verified on Base.
